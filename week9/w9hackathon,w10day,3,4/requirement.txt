📌 Objective
Enhance the healthcare products chatbot so that users can describe their symptoms in natural language (typed or spoken), and the AI will suggest relevant product categories or supplements.

---

🔑 Features to Implement

1. Symptom Input
  * User can type or speak their symptoms (using yesterday’s speech-to-text).
  * Example: “I feel tired and weak”, “I’m losing hair”, “My bones are fragile”.
2. AI Symptom → Product Mapping
  * Use LangGraph/OpenAI to analyze the symptom text.
  * Map symptoms to product categories in MongoDB (e.g., tired → iron, B-complex vitamins; hair loss → biotin, zinc).
  * If multiple possible matches → suggest top 2–3 with a short reasoning.
3. Result Display in Chatbot
  * Chatbot replies with:
    * Suggested product(s).
    * Short explanation: “Hair fall is often linked to Biotin or Zinc deficiency. Here are some supplements you can try.”
  * Show clickable product cards (from MongoDB) inside the chat response.

---

🧑‍💻 Tech Stack

* Frontend (Next.js)
  * Reuse chatbot UI from Day 1.
  * Add a dedicated prompt: “Describe your symptoms”.
  * If voice input used → auto-feed transcription into chatbot.
* Backend (Nest.js)
  * New endpoint /symptom-checker that takes symptom text → calls AI → returns mapped products.
  * Use LangGraph/OpenAI for intent classification + reasoning.
* Database (MongoDB)
  * Products already exist in DB.
  * Add a small symptom-to-category mapping JSON for better accuracy. Example:

{
  "tired": ["Vitamin B Complex", "Iron Supplements"],
  "hair fall": ["Biotin", "Zinc", "Multivitamin"],
  "weak bones": ["Calcium", "Vitamin D"],
  "stress": ["Magnesium", "Ashwagandha"]
}


---

📲 Example Flow

1. User clicks 🎤 and says: “I feel tired all day and have low energy.”
2. Speech-to-text → "I feel tired all day and have low energy."
3. AI processes symptoms → matches "tired" + "low energy" → suggests Vitamin B Complex, Iron.
4. Chatbot reply:
5. “Fatigue and low energy are often linked to Vitamin B and Iron deficiencies. I recommend trying these supplements 👇”
6. (Show product cards from MongoDB)
